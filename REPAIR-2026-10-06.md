# BigBasket retry repair — October 6, 2026

Status: locally repaired and tested; not yet deployed. Public build remains 1.0.24.

## Evidence

The consented customer-run table is empty, so its input, build and error are

The explicitly approved diagnostic used public build 1.0.24, five milk products,

The diagnostic did not use or alter any customer history store.

## Change

The old loop starts every page at proxy session suffix 1, even when another
session succeeded. It also retains failed session cookies for reuse on later
pages and sources. The repaired loader retains the successful anonymous session,
retires it on request failure, and gives retries fresh proxy session IDs/cookies.
Direct mode resets failed cookies without enabling a proxy. Attempts remain
bounded at three per page; there is no extra delay after the final failed attempt.

Location guard errors still stop immediately. Output, filters, tracking keys,
atomic charging, prices, RAM and default inputs remain unchanged. This improves
recovery but cannot guarantee that BigBasket will accept every request.
